# Skyrun – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 17, 2026

## 1. The short version

Skyrun does not collect, sell or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no trackers. Your progress stays on your own device.

## 2. What we store on your device

To remember your game, Skyrun saves a small amount of data in your browser's local storage, such as:

- High scores and coins
- Unlocked skins and upgrades
- Settings like sound and quality
- Redeemed codes and event progress

This data never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to any server.

## 3. What we don't collect

- Your name, email or phone number
- Your location
- Your contacts, photos or files
- Advertising IDs or analytics data

## 4. Works offline

Skyrun is a single, self-contained file. Graphics, sounds and fonts are all built in, so the game doesn't connect to the internet while you play.

## 5. Children

Skyrun is safe for players of all ages. Because the game collects no personal data, no information about children is ever gathered.

## 6. Deleting your data

You're in control. Clearing your browser's site data, or uninstalling the game, removes all saved progress permanently.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted with a new "Last updated" date.
